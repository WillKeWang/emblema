VI · The Wheel of the Orrery

Conductor · broad function · system-initiated
Tarot · Wheel of Fortune Instrument · Orrery Scope: broad Initiative: system
"Sponte sua, ad horam" — "Of itself, at the appointed hour."

What kind of AI agent

A broad function that runs on the system's own schedule. The system holds the cycle; the user sets the conditions and reviews the output.

The Tarot archetype

The Wheel of Fortune turns by itself; we ride. The card is about cyclical, system-driven action — not random fortune in the modern sense but the older fortuna, the patterned recurrence of work. Things happen on the Wheel's schedule, not yours.

The scientific instrument

The orrery — a mechanical model of the solar system, driven by clockwork, running continuously and cyclically. The orrery is the canonical instrument of scheduled astronomy — used in 18th-century education and prediction. Its gears do not measure once; they keep measuring.

Real-world examples

  • CI bots that run the test suite and write a summary comment after every commit.
  • Scheduled experiment runners that surface reports on a cron.
  • Drift-detection pipelines that re-run the eval set on new data.
  • Automated dataset validators; recurring reproducibility checks.

Strength

Ensures broad subtasks happen even when you forget. Time-shifted work is the Wheel's specialty.

Signature failure mode

Takes broad action you didn't sanction. If the phase scope drifts (new data, new code), the Conductor runs the wrong phase to completion before you notice. Cycles assume their inputs; when inputs change quietly, the output is silently wrong.

Philosophical commitment

Hutchins' distributed cognition (Cognition in the Wild, 1995): the cognitive system extends beyond any one agent, and the Wheel is the part of the system that remembers on your behalf. Norbert Wiener's cybernetic feedback loop sits behind — the broad task as a controlled cycle with the human as supervisor.

The kind of co-scientist that fits you

Pipelines, automated experiment runners, monitoring agents with a clear pause/resume control. The system surface should declare its schedule; the human should hold the kill switch.
