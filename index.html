The two axes, defined

Axis Definition Survey signal
Scope How much of a task the AI handles in one invocation. Narrow (a single function call) → Broad (a coherent multi-step subtask) → Whole (end-to-end goal pursuit). Q3 (current uses) and Q10 (what would justify setup), averaged. Q1c competence (more competent → narrower) and Q2 frequency (daily → broader) modulate.
Initiative Who proposes the next move. User-initiated (system only responds when invoked) → Mixed (either party can propose) → System-initiated (system drives, user reacts). Q11 (top desired qualities), Q4 (current frustrations), Q10 contributions, summed. Q1c and Q2 modulate.

Honest notes on the typology

  • Cells I (Magician) through VI (Wheel) describe AI tools that exist in current daily research practice.
  • Cell VIII (Priestess) is the survey's central aspirational target — the "AI co-scientist" as currently positioned by Sakana, FutureHouse, and Google AI Co-Scientist.
  • Cells VII (Fool) and IX (Hermit) describe whole-task agency: VII as currently shipping autonomous-agent products (Devin, Manus); IX as mostly aspirational, requiring trust infrastructure that does not yet exist.
  • The typology is descriptive, not prescriptive — the goal is to make different positions toward AI tools legible and discussable, not to rank them.
