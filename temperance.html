II · Temperance of the Sundial

Adjuster · narrow function · mixed-initiated
Tarot · Temperance Instrument · Sundial Scope: narrow Initiative: mixed
"Mensura ante actum" — "Measure before action."

What kind of AI agent

You reach for AI for narrow operations, but you want the system to push back inside the operation — to ask a clarifying question, flag an ambiguity, or propose an alternative before committing. The combination of narrow scope and mixed initiative means you accept friction in exchange for the system catching mistakes you would have made. The pragmatic recommendation today is conversational LLMs in clarification mode — Claude or ChatGPT prompted to ask one question before answering, smart autocomplete that suggests reformulations, spell- and type-checkers that propose rather than impose. The friction must be calibrated to the cost of the underlying operation: high-friction for high-stakes calls, low-friction for routine ones.

The Tarot archetype

Temperance is the angel pouring liquid between two vessels — the act of moderating exchange. The card represents calibration, the patient art of the right proportion. The angel does not act alone and does not let the user act alone; she holds the moment in suspension.

The scientific instrument

The sundial — a time-measurement instrument calibrated to the local meridian. The gnomon's shadow encodes solar position; the hour-marks anchor the reading. The sundial is the clock as reference: it does not advance; it discloses where you already are. Calibration is its whole nature.

Real-world examples

  • An autocomplete that asks "did you mean X?"
  • A search reformulator suggesting refinements before running.
  • A spell-corrector that proposes rather than imposes.
  • An LLM that asks one clarifying question before answering a narrow request.

Strength

Catches user mistakes inside the operation, without leaving the operation. The tightest reasonable feedback loop.

Signature failure mode

Pestering. The Adjuster's check overrides correct user intent when its heuristic is wrong — especially in domains the model misreads (technical vocabulary, non-English names, intentional unconventional usage). Mis-calibrated friction is worse than none.

Philosophical commitment

Buber's I-Thou in miniature: the tool encountered as a quasi-other capable of response within a defined exchange. A turn of conversation where each side keeps half the agency. Polanyi's observation that we know more than we can tell (The Tacit Dimension, 1966) is the latent justification — the system asks because the user's full intent is rarely fully spelled out.

How you got here

The survey routes you to this card based on the following signals:

  • Q3 dominated by narrow operations (coding, writing, figures) — scope score in the 1.3–1.7 range.
  • Q11 mixes "Correctness" or "Clear explanation of uncertainty" with operationally-balanced choices.
  • Q4 frustrations frequently include "overconfident answers" or "misunderstanding dataset" — but the user is willing to accept friction in exchange for the check.
  • Often the largest cluster in the pilot — researchers using AI for narrow daily work and asking for more checks, not more autonomy.
