"Mensura ante actum" — "Measure before action."

What kind of AI agent

The angel on the Temperance card adjusts the flow between two vessels — a light touch, but the point is whether the proportion is right. The sundial works the same way: it doesn't push you forward, it only shows you where you are right now.

Your use of AI is still narrow, but you're willing to let it calibrate alongside you on small tasks. You might ask Claude to revise an email, and it asks first whether you want to sound more formal or more friendly. You might ask ChatGPT to rewrite an explanation, and it flags that a concept might be misread by your audience.

Behind this is a kind of careful willingness to collaborate. You don't want to hand everything off, but you also don't want to carry every small judgment alone. Often you already have a sense of the answer — you just want one confirmation. You tend to think ahead to what could go wrong: is the phrasing accurate enough, is there a detail missing, could a small decision here create extra cost later. So before sending the email, before submitting the document, before moving to the next step, you let AI take one more look.

That need often comes from caring about quality. You've encountered AI giving an answer that sounded plausible but got the details completely wrong, or misreading your data while appearing confident. That experience made you want it to confirm direction before it acts. You accept friction inside the operation because the cost is usually lower than fixing things afterward.

But friction has a limit. If every small action requires going back and forth three times, this way of working starts to drain you. What you want is exactly one well-placed pause — no more than that.

The Tarot archetype

Temperance is the angel pouring liquid between two vessels — the act of moderating exchange. The card represents calibration, the patient art of the right proportion. The angel does not act alone and does not let the user act alone; she holds the moment in suspension.

The scientific instrument

The sundial — a time-measurement instrument calibrated to the local meridian. The gnomon's shadow encodes solar position; the hour-marks anchor the reading. The sundial is the clock as reference: it does not advance; it discloses where you already are. Calibration is its whole nature.

Strength

Catches user mistakes inside the operation, without leaving the operation. The tightest reasonable feedback loop.

Signature failure mode

Pestering. The Adjuster's check overrides correct user intent when its heuristic is wrong — especially in domains the model misreads (technical vocabulary, non-English names, intentional unconventional usage). Mis-calibrated friction is worse than none.

How you got here

The survey routes you to this card based on the following signals:

  • Q3 dominated by narrow operations (coding, writing, figures) — scope score in the 1.3–1.7 range.
  • Q11 mixes "Correctness" or "Clear explanation of uncertainty" with operationally-balanced choices.
  • Q4 frustrations frequently include "overconfident answers" or "misunderstanding dataset" — but the user is willing to accept friction in exchange for the check.
  • Often the largest cluster in the pilot — researchers using AI for narrow daily work and asking for more checks, not more autonomy.
