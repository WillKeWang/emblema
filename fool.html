VII · The Fool of the Sextant

Deputy · whole task · user-initiated
Tarot · The Fool Instrument · Sextant Scope: whole Initiative: user
"Dimissus cum instrumento" — "Sent forth with the instrument."

What kind of AI agent

You take on whole-project research — multi-stage work from data through manuscript — but you want to drive every decision yourself, with AI as a step-by-step assistant rather than an autonomous agent. The combination of whole-task scope and strong user-initiative means that specialized co-scientist systems are the wrong fit for you: they offer more autonomy than you want, and their setup cost buys you capabilities you'd rather supply yourself. The pragmatic recommendation today is a generic LLM — Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor — used as a thinking partner that you micromanage: you supply the structure, the schema, the priorities, and the verification at every step; the AI helps with the local effort under your tight direction. No system delegates the project; you do the project, and AI accompanies.

The Tarot archetype

The Fool sets out alone with a single bundle, looking at the horizon, not the void. The card is about the journey itself — taking on the whole road and accepting that no one else will walk it for you. It is not foolishness; it is the willingness to be the one acting at every step.

The scientific instrument

The sextant — a celestial-navigation instrument (Hadley, 1731) for measuring the angle between a star and the horizon. With a chronometer and a star almanac it gives the navigator latitude and longitude. The sextant is the chosen instrument the navigator carries: the journey is theirs to make, the sextant is leverage on each fix along the way. Harrison's longitude solution required one fix at a time, each one read and decided by a person.

Real-world examples

  • Claude or ChatGPT used as a whole-PhD-project assistant, with every step verified.
  • Cursor used in agent mode with constant interrupt-and-override at each commit.
  • Generic LLMs used as research-thinking partners across multi-month projects, with the human supplying all structural decisions.

Strength

Maximum user agency at whole-project scope. You stay the decision-maker throughout, and nothing happens that you did not choose.

Signature failure mode

Maximum cognitive load on the user. Because no system delegates, every structural decision — what to do next, what to verify, what to prioritize — has to come from you. The Fool's failure is not drift but exhaustion.
"I want there to be friction between these tools and my project so that all decisions have to be verified." — pilot Q12 free text

Philosophical commitment

Mollick's centaur (Co-Intelligence, 2024) extended to whole-project scope: the rider stays the rider through every leg of the journey, with the AI as the mount that gives reach but never the reins. Polanyi's tacit knowledge sits behind: the structure of a research project cannot be fully specified to an agent in advance, so the structure must stay with the researcher who holds it tacitly.
