VII · The Fool of the Sextant

Deputy · whole task · user-initiated
Tarot · The Fool Instrument · Sextant Scope: whole Initiative: user
"Dimissus cum instrumento" — "Sent forth with the instrument."

What kind of AI agent

A fully autonomous agent given a charge. The user names the goal; the agent attempts the whole project, returning with the result.

The Tarot archetype

The Fool steps off the cliff with a single bundle, looking at the horizon, not the void. The card is about the leap of action — accepting the whole charge with limited information. It is not foolishness; it is the willingness to act before knowing.

The scientific instrument

The sextant — a celestial-navigation instrument (Hadley, 1731) for measuring the angle between a star and the horizon. With a chronometer and a star almanac it gives you latitude and longitude. The sextant is the instrument of the single fix: one observation, one position. Harrison's longitude solution rested on one such fix at a time.

Real-world examples

  • Devin / Cognition; Manus.
  • AutoGPT-style agents pointed at a project goal.
  • Claude with extended autonomous "build me an X" workflows.

Strength

Maximum offload. The "I just want it done" mode.

Signature failure mode

Maximum drift. The user does not see the middle, so failures compound silently.
"I want there to be friction between these tools and my project so that all decisions have to be verified." — pilot Q12 free textThe Fool walks the cliff edge; sometimes the cliff is real.

Philosophical commitment

Hannah Arendt's distinction between labor, work, and action (The Human Condition, 1958) — the Deputy is the agent of action, the irreducible undertaking of a project whose result cannot be guaranteed in advance. The card carries Arendt's central insight: action is the form of freedom that begins something new, and is therefore irrevocable.

The kind of co-scientist that fits you

End-to-end agents you delegate to and trust to return with a chronicle of what they did, not just the output. The Fool's card asks for the bundle, not just the destination.
