III · Justice of the Plumb-Line

Inspector · narrow function · system-initiated
Tarot · Justice Instrument · Plumb-line Scope: narrow Initiative: system
"Quod deviat, signo" — "What deviates, I mark."

What kind of AI agent

Justice sits quietly. She speaks only when she finds a deviation — to point out where you need to look back.

You save the file and a red underline appears under a variable three lines up: a typo you would not have caught on this pass. You did not ask it to look. The checker was watching the whole time.

You do not want AI to think for you. You know your attention is limited, so you let the system watch low-level details like type errors, abnormal experiment results, or data pipeline issues. These things matter, but they should not occupy your mind all the time. AI feels valuable when it catches something you missed, but only if it stays quiet most of the time and speaks when something truly needs attention.

The Tarot archetype

Justice sits frontally with scales and sword, watchful and impersonal. She is the card of discriminating attention, what is true, what deviant, what must be marked. Justice does not punish. She flags.

The scientific instrument

The plumb-line, a weighted cord defining true vertical, used in surveying, geodesy, and gravity science. Eratosthenes used analogous measurements to calculate Earth's circumference. The plumb is a passive reference. It does not measure, it discloses what aligns and what doesn't.

Where to start

  • Turn on the validators you already have but ignore. A linter, a type-checker, a spell-checker. Let them run on every save instead of on demand.
  • Add one check for an error that has actually bitten you. A data-drift alert on an input file, a pre-commit hook, a CI check that flags a changed result.
  • Guard the signal. The moment you start ignoring the squiggles, the system has failed. Tune the rules down to the errors you genuinely want flagged, so a real one never hides in the noise.

Strength

Catches small errors continuously, freeing the user from the burden of remembering to check.

Signature failure mode

Alert fatigue. The Inspector's true positives get drowned by false ones, and eventually the user stops reading the squiggles and a real error slips through. Alert fatigue is the Sentinel's failure mode in miniature.

What's at stake

Most of the time a good tool is invisible. You look straight through it at the work. Justice is the tool designed to become visible at one specific moment, the instant something is wrong, and only then. That is the whole idea. It makes the error stand out precisely because everything else has faded into the background. The risk is a failure of attention. When the flags come too often, or for things that are not really wrong, you stop seeing them, and the one that mattered slips past with the rest.

Background reading. Heidegger on the present-at-hand, the tool that stops withdrawing from awareness and becomes conspicuous at the moment of breakdown (Being and Time, §16).

How you got here

The survey routes you to this card based on the following signals:

  • Q3 dominated by narrow daily operations.
  • Q11 emphasizes "Experiment tracking" and "Reproducibility" alongside the daily-coding qualities, pulling initiative toward the system.
  • Q10 (setup-burden willingness) often shows "It catches mistakes I would otherwise miss" as a primary justification.

A snapshot, not a verdict. This card reflects how you work with AI tools right now, which is shaped as much by what you have had access to as by what you would choose. Justice is often a quiet layer running underneath whatever other card describes your main way of working; whether it is your whole stance depends a lot on how far you have tuned your tools.
