III · Justice of the Plumb-Line

Inspector · narrow function · system-initiated
Tarot · Justice Instrument · Plumb-line Scope: narrow Initiative: system
"Quod deviat, signo" — "What deviates, I mark."

What kind of AI agent

You want AI to run continuously alongside your work and surface small errors as they appear — without being asked, and silent when things are right. The combination of narrow scope and system-initiative means you trust the system to flag concrete issues at the operation level, and you tolerate it being quiet otherwise. The pragmatic recommendation today is continuous validators — linters (ruff, eslint), type-checkers (mypy, ts), spell-checkers, security scanners running on save, real-time syntax highlighting, drift detectors on incoming data. The discipline is signal-to-noise: the system defaults to silence and breaks it only with high confidence in the deviation.

The Tarot archetype

Justice sits frontally with scales and sword, watchful and impersonal. She is the card of discriminating attention — what is true, what deviant, what must be marked. Justice does not punish; she flags.

The scientific instrument

The plumb-line — a weighted cord defining true vertical, used in surveying, geodesy, and gravity science. Eratosthenes used analogous measurements to calculate Earth's circumference. The plumb is a passive reference: it does not measure, it discloses what aligns and what doesn't.

Real-world examples

  • Linters (ruff, eslint), type-checkers (mypy, ts), spell-checkers, real-time syntax validators.
  • Security scanners running on save.
  • Drift-detection on incoming data; pre-commit hooks; CI checks that surface deltas.

Strength

Catches small errors continuously, freeing the user from the burden of remembering to check.

Signature failure mode

Alert fatigue. The Inspector's true positives get drowned by false ones; eventually the user stops reading the squiggles and a real error slips through. Alert fatigue is the Sentinel's failure mode in miniature.

Philosophical commitment

Heidegger's vorhanden — the tool that stops withdrawing the moment something is wrong, becoming visibly present at the breakdown (Being and Time, §16). The Inspector is the operationalization of breakdown-as-feature: it makes the wrong thing visible exactly because everything else withdraws into the background.
