III · Justice of the Plumb-Line

Inspector · narrow function · system-initiated
Tarot · Justice Instrument · Plumb-line Scope: narrow Initiative: system
"Quod deviat, signo" — "What deviates, I mark."

What kind of AI agent

Justice sits quietly. She doesn't do the work for you, and she doesn't keep talking. She speaks only when she finds a deviation — to point out where you need to look back.

You might run ruff in VS Code, so it flags problems as they appear. You might also want a system that automatically checks after each experiment run whether the results have drifted from the historical baseline; that looks for distribution shift when a new batch of data arrives; that confirms before you commit that the handling logic for key variables hasn't changed unexpectedly. You don't need to go looking. You just need it to tell you when something's wrong.

Behind this is usually a kind of protection for working memory. It's not that you want AI to think for you — it's that your attention is limited, and you don't want to spend your main effort repeatedly scanning low-level details: type errors in code, whether experiment outputs contain unexpected values, whether a data pipeline has quietly introduced new missing entries. These things matter, but they're not worth occupying your mind. You're willing to let the system watch them so you can keep your attention on the parts that actually require judgment.

So when AI proactively catches a problem you didn't notice, you find it valuable. But your standard for alerts is high. A good system should be quiet most of the time and only speak when it has genuinely found something wrong. Too few alerts and there's no point; too many and it becomes noise. Justice's strength comes from restraint, not from speaking often.

The Tarot archetype

Justice sits frontally with scales and sword, watchful and impersonal. She is the card of discriminating attention — what is true, what deviant, what must be marked. Justice does not punish; she flags.

The scientific instrument

The plumb-line — a weighted cord defining true vertical, used in surveying, geodesy, and gravity science. Eratosthenes used analogous measurements to calculate Earth's circumference. The plumb is a passive reference: it does not measure, it discloses what aligns and what doesn't.

Strength

Catches small errors continuously, freeing the user from the burden of remembering to check.

Signature failure mode

Alert fatigue. The Inspector's true positives get drowned by false ones; eventually the user stops reading the squiggles and a real error slips through. Alert fatigue is the Sentinel's failure mode in miniature.
